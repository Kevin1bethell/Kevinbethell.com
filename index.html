I am currently teaching as visiting faculty at Ohio University and as an assistant lecturer at the University of Akron. I am also a Ph.D. student in Ohio University's Interdisciplinary Arts program. My work primarily explores aesthetic and ethical questions surrounding body art, particularly tattooing.

Current research

Tattooing at the intersection of aesthetics and asceticism

Across cultures, ascetic traditions have long employed bodily pain, discipline, and ritual as means to transcendence and knowledge. My dissertation examines tattooing alongside this broader history of aesthetic and ascetic practices, with particular attention to embodiment, pain, ritual, and aesthetic meaning.
