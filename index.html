Philosophical interests

Aesthetics, embodiment, and the history of philosophy.

My primary interests are in aesthetics and philosophy of art, especially questions surrounding body art, tattooing, embodiment, artistic practice, and aesthetic experience. I am particularly interested in cases that complicate familiar distinctions between artwork and experience, artist and spectator, aesthetic value and bodily transformation.

Much of my work develops through sustained engagement with Kant, Nietzsche, and German Idealism, alongside figures such as Gadamer, Agamben, Dewey, and Spinoza. I am interested both in the history of aesthetic theory and in what these traditions can contribute to contemporary philosophical problems.

My broader philosophical interests include ethics, the relationship between aesthetics and ethics, asceticism, philosophy of religion, interpretation and hermeneutics, and questions of artistic agency and authorship—including those raised by AI-generated art.
